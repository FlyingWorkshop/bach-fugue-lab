How it's made

Notes come from Humdrum **kern editions, which spell every accidental out instead of leaving it to a key signature. Verovio engraves them at build time and every notehead's position is lifted out of the engraved SVG, so the score, the roll and the audio cannot drift apart.

The entries, strettos, episodes and key changes are all measured from the score. The one thing sometimes typed in is where the subject ends: the detector gets 32 of the 58 right on its own and the other 26 are set by hand, which each fugue's notes panel tells you. About a thousand lines of Python, in the repository.

None of this is scholarship — a program decides what counts as an entry, so it misses statements Bach buries in the texture and brackets a few a musicologist would call something else. If you want an analysis you can rely on, go to the literature.

The **kern encodings are by David Huron and Craig Stuart Sapp — bach-wtc-fugues, art-of-the-fugue. Neither has a licence file, so the build fetches them from upstream rather than redistributing them. The engraved edition underneath is the Bach-Gesellschaft (1866).
